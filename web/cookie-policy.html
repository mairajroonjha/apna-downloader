Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and Local Storage are small text data files stored in your web browser by websites you visit. They allow websites to remember your session login state and user preferences (such as dark or light theme).

2. Strictly Necessary Functional Storage Only

Apna Downloader uses only strictly necessary functional storage. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts:

Storage Key / Name Type Purpose Duration
authToken Local Storage Stores your secure customer portal login session token. Session / 30 Days
theme Local Storage Remembers your visual theme preference (Dark or Light Mode). Persistent

3. No Third-Party Tracking or Analytics Cookies

We do NOT use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, marketing cookies, or third-party behavioral profiling cookies. Your browsing activity on Apna Downloader remains completely private.

4. Managing Storage Preferences

Because we only use strictly necessary functional cookies for login authentication and theme display, disabling local storage in your browser settings will log you out of the customer portal and reset theme preferences.
