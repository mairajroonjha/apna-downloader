Account & Desktop Setup

  • Click **Customer Portal** in the navigation header to register or log in.
  • Download and launch the Windows Installer (`.exe`) from the Download Center.
  • Log in to the desktop app using the same registered email credentials.
02

Install Browser Extension

  • Download the `browser-extension.zip` file and extract it on your computer.
  • Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/` URL address.
  • Turn on **Developer Mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
  • Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left and select the extracted folder.
03

Purchase & Activation

  • Log in to the **Customer Portal** and navigate to **Upgrade License**.
  • Select a plan and transfer the payment to **EasyPaisa** or **NayaPay** (using numbers or QR codes).
  • Submit your 11-digit Transaction ID (TID) along with the transfer receipt.
  • Your license will activate automatically within 1-2 hours upon admin approval!

Help & Support Center

Frequently asked questions and guides about license management.

How do I request a custom email discount?

Our administrators can issue special promotional campaign discounts for specific email addresses from the admin panel. Once configured, you will see a personalized gift icon and reduced checkout rates when logging into the portal!

What are PC License slots?

Each subscription has a device allocation limit (e.g. 1 PC, 2 PCs, 3 PCs). If you reach your limit, you can easily release/unbind old device IDs from your online Customer Portal to register a new laptop or computer.

Can I pay in local currency (Pakistani Rupees)?

Yes! All our packages are priced in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). We support local payments including Easypaisa, JazzCash, Nayapay, and bank transfers for instant manual checkout validations.
