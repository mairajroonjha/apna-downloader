Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Apna Downloader ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our desktop download manager application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential data required to maintain your account and validate software licenses:

    Account Credentials: Email address and hashed password when you create an account on our customer portal. Device Binding Data: Unique non-reversible hardware hash of your Windows PC used solely for seat allocation and device slot license verification. Payment Receipts: Transaction IDs (TID) and payment verification receipts submitted for manual billing approval.

3. Data We DO NOT Collect

We strongly value privacy in file transfers. Apna Downloader does NOT track, log, or transmit:

    Your downloaded file contents, file names, or storage directories. Your target download URLs or browsing activity history. Personal files or data stored on your hard drive.

4. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is exclusively used to:

    Authenticate your customer account login and manage active device seats. Verify manual payment submissions and activate license subscriptions. Send essential system transactional notifications (e.g. trial extension, subscription confirmation).

5. Data Security & Storage

Your account data is secured using industry-standard encryption protocols. Passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashes. Database records are protected behind Cloudflare edge firewall security.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at mirajroonjha@gmail.com.
